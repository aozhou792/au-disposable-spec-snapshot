# Sealed disposable disposal checklist (Australia)

**Scope:** A finished or damaged sealed disposable, including the Alibarbar Ingot 9000 (2350 mAh lithium cell, no charge port).
**Canonical:** https://www.alibarbarigetvape.com/research/high-puff-disposable-capacity-australia-2026
**Adults 18+.** General information, not medical or legal advice, and not a substitute for the collection point's own rules.

A finished device still contains a lithium battery.

- [ ] Do not put it in household rubbish.
- [ ] Do not put it in the kerbside recycling bin.
- [ ] Hand it in whole at an e-waste or battery drop-off. Ask the local council which site accepts it.
- [ ] Do not open the device to remove the battery.
- [ ] Keep finished units together, cool, and away from loose metal, children and pets, until drop-off.
- [ ] Swelling, unusual heat, a burning or chemical smell, leaking liquid, hissing or smoke: stop using it.
- [ ] If it is hot and safe to move, set it on concrete, tile or a metal tray, away from anything that can burn. Do not cool it in water.
- [ ] If it is smoking or on fire, keep clear and call 000.
- [ ] Liquid on skin: wash with soap and water. If someone may have swallowed liquid, call the Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26.
- [ ] Tell drop-off staff when a unit is swollen, leaking or damaged.
- [ ] Airlines generally require lithium devices in cabin baggage, not checked bags. Check that airline before flying.
- [ ] Store unused devices cool and dry, not in a hot car, not in direct sun, and not loose with keys or coins.
