# Ingot 9000 LED readings

**Device:** Alibarbar Ingot 9000 (sealed, non-rechargeable).
**Hardware reviewed:** 2026-08-20.
**Canonical:** https://www.alibarbarigetvape.com/research/high-puff-disposable-capacity-australia-2026
**Adults 18+.** Exact flash patterns belong to the device in hand. This note does not publish a blink code.

The LED is two readings, not one: battery still left, and e-liquid still left.

| What you see | What it means here |
|--------------|--------------------|
| E-liquid empty, battery still showing charge | Finished. Replace the device. |
| Battery empty and vapour falling away | Finished. Do not charge it. There is no charge port. |
| Flash with little or no vapour | Usual end-of-life sign. Stop drawing on it. |
| Burnt taste, or vapour that thins suddenly | Usual end-of-life sign, including when the icon is not a neat "empty". |
| Healthy readings and normal vapour | Keep using it. Line up a spare before a long day if the bars are already low. |

Do not open the case, puncture it, or jury-rig a charger.

When the device is finished it still holds a lithium cell. See [disposal-checklist.md](./disposal-checklist.md).
